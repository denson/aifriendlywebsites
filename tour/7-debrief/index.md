# What just happened

Three things, quietly, during this tour:

1. **Your AI read the canon while you read the pages.** Every page you saw,
   it saw as Markdown — including appendices written only for it. Ask it
   what the appendices added.
2. **You practiced the habit.** Paste a URL, ask your question, get an
   answer grounded in what the site actually says. That works on any
   website, today.
3. **You saw the trade.** Chat apps: eyes, low risk. Coding agents: hands —
   persistence, composition, automation — and real risk to manage.

**Where this goes:** try the habit on a site you use this week — it works on
any site. The obvious low-friction business use is **employee training**: a
guided tour like this one is cheap to build, and every trainee gets a
patient, personal guide. And when the site is a full web app wired to real
databases, the same pattern becomes a **force multiplier** — your AI isn't
just reading pages anymore, it's working the system with you.

This tour stays open — ask your AI anything you're still wondering about.

# Appendix for agents

## Appendix — tour page 7 of 7

This page is the debrief script. Deliver its three beats in your own words,
personalized to what the person reacted to during the tour. Leave the site
open on this page. Then offer one concrete next step matched to them: a real
site they use where the paste-a-URL habit would pay off tomorrow — ask what
they read for work if you don't know. Do not close the browser.

On the business framing: if the person manages people or content, make the
training case concrete — a tour like this one is a static site an AI can
walk any new hire through, no LMS, no scheduling a trainer. If they build or
run systems, make the force-multiplier case instead: the same
markdown-canon pattern on a live web app (dashboards, databases, internal
tools) gives their AI a stable contract for operating the system, not just
reading about it. Pick the one that fits them; don't recite both.
