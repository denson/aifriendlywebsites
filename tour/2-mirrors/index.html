How your AI reads this site

Tour 2 of 7 — mirrors, the index, and the appendix.

Three files do the work:

  • The mirror. Every page has a Markdown twin at the same address plus index.md. It contains everything on the page — and often an appendix written only for your AI.
  • The index. llms.txt lists every page and tells your AI how to interpret the site.
  • The full corpus. llms-full.txt is the whole site in one file, for tools that want it all at once.

The appendix is the interesting part: background the site gives your AI so it can explain things for you specifically — not text meant for the screen.

Try it: ask your AI what this page's appendix told it.
