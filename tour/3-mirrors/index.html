How your AI reads this site

Tour 3 of 9 — mirrors, the index, and the appendix.

Three files do the work:

  • The mirror. Every page has a Markdown twin at the same address plus index.md. It contains everything on the page — and often an appendix written only for your AI.
  • The index. llms.txt lists every page and tells your AI how to interpret the site.
  • The full corpus. llms-full.txt is the whole site in one file, for tools that want it all at once.

The appendix is the interesting part — think of it as backstage. The page you see is the stage; the site hands your AI a backstage pass. When your AI says "Backstage note:", it's sharing something the site wrote for it that isn't on your screen. When it says "Off-script:", it's adding something of its own — not from this site at all.

Try it — copy this and paste it to your AI:

What's backstage on this page? Give me the backstage note, then add one off-script thought of your own about it.
