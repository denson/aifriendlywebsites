Tour 8 of 8 — the debrief, and where this goes next.

Three things, quietly, during this tour:

  1. Your AI read the canon while you read the pages. Every page you saw, it saw as Markdown — including appendices written only for it. Ask it what the appendices added.
  2. You practiced the habit. Paste a URL, ask your question, get an answer grounded in what the site says. Works on much of the web — and where it doesn't is exactly why sites like this matter.
  3. You saw the trade. Chat apps: eyes, low risk. Coding agents: hands — persistence, composition, automation — and real risk to manage.

Where this goes: try the habit on a site you use this week. The obvious low-friction business use is employee training: your trainers build tours like this themselves, so every trainee gets the trainer's own guidance on demand — the trainer stays the author and the authority; the site multiplies their reach. And when the site is a full web app wired to real databases, the pattern becomes a force multiplier — your AI isn't just reading pages, it's working the system with you.

The tour stays open — ask your AI anything else.
