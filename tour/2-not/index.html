What it is not

Tour 2 of 9 — the locked-down web, and why being AI-friendly is still rare.

Most of the web is not built this way, and it's worth understanding why before you see how this site works. Around AI there is real fear, plus plenty of misinformation and disinformation — and the knee-jerk response is the lockdown: block every AI agent, even one a human is directly guiding.

The lockdown backfires. The rules that govern polite AI — robots.txt, an internet standard with three decades of practice, and in the EU legally binding for AI training — are followed by exactly the agents you'd want to welcome. Attackers using AI ignore the rules and punch through barriers, which can itself be a crime. So a lockdown filters out the lawful visitors and barely inconveniences the rest.

AI-friendly is the alternative: publish for both readerships, openly, on your own terms — which is what the rest of this tour shows you.
