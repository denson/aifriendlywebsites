The habit

Tour 4 of 9 — hand pages to your AI and ask your question.

When a website matters to you, don't just read it — hand it to your AI with your question:

  • "Does this apply to my situation? Here's my situation: …"
  • "Compare what this page says with that other page."
  • "Explain this like I'm new to the topic."
  • "What does this site say it will not do?"

The point isn't summary. It's your question against their content, answered from what the site actually says instead of what your AI half-remembers.

This works on much of the web — but not all of it, and the difference is the next page: some sites limit AI agents, and some actively fight them.
