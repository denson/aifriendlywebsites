What is an AI-friendly website?

Tour 1 of 9 — the idea in one screen.

Most websites are written for human eyes. When an AI reads one, it scrapes the page and guesses at the structure — and loses whatever the design buried.

An AI-friendly website is built the other way around. Every page starts as a clean, structured version written to be read and understood by AI — no visual clutter, and carrying as much extra information for agents as the site wants to include. The web page you see is generated from it: the human page is the subset, the AI's version is the canon.

The agent-readable side can be as simple as Markdown — a plain text file — or as deep as an enterprise system like Salesforce or Google Earth Engine answering your AI directly. Structures like knowledge graphs sit along that same range and can even help a weak AI reason better. This page is the demo: it has a machine-readable twin your AI is reading right now.
