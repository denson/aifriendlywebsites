Why other sites answer worse

Tour 4 of 8 — the spectrum from agent-friendly to agent-hostile.

Same habit, wildly different results — because sites sit on a spectrum:

Kind of site What your agent gets
AI-friendly (this site) Clean canon: mirrors, llms.txt, open access
Indifferent (most sites) Scraped HTML — works, with more guessing
Limiting robots.txt tells your agent to stay out
Hostile Bot-walls that block, trap, or even break your agent
  • robots.txt refusal — your agent won't fetch a page that loads fine for you: politeness, not breakage.
  • Bot-wall ("verify you are human") — agents can't pass these; at the extreme one can crash the AI app itself. Stop the retries; open the site in your own browser.

This site does everything on the friendly end — no walls, no gates, open access, a mirror beside every page. That's the difference you feel.
