# More power

Chat apps give your AI eyes. Coding agents — **Claude Code** and OpenAI's
**Codex** — give it hands. They read this site the same way, but they can
also *keep* and *use* what they read:

- **Persistence** — "save this site's mirrors into my notes and keep them
  updated."
- **Composition** — "read this site's guide and apply it to my project."
- **Automation** — "each morning, fetch these three sites' llms.txt and tell
  me what changed."

AI-friendly sites make this reliable: an agent automating against scraped
HTML breaks when the design changes; one reading published Markdown is
reading a stable contract.
