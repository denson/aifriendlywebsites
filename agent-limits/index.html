How sites limit your agent

The spectrum from agent-friendly to agent-hostile — what robots.txt and llms.txt can do to your AI, and where bot-walls break it.

"Paste a URL, ask your question" works on much of the web. Not all. Sites sit on a spectrum, and it helps to know which kind you're on:

Kind of site What your agent gets
AI-friendly (this site) Clean canon: mirrors, llms.txt, open access
Indifferent (most sites) Scraped HTML — works, with more guessing
Limiting robots.txt tells your agent to stay out
Hostile Bot-walls that block, trap, or even break your agent

What robots.txt can do to your agent

robots.txt is a file a site publishes saying which automated visitors are welcome. It has no enforcement of its own — but well-behaved agents enforce it against themselves. If a site's robots.txt disallows AI agents (or everyone), your AI's fetch tool will refuse the page and report it, even though the same page loads fine in your own browser. That's your agent being polite, not broken.

What bot-detection walls can do to your agent

Some sites sit behind bot-protection services whose challenge pages ("verify you are human") decide whether a visitor looks automated. Agents can't pass these — and shouldn't try. Effects range from a blocked fetch, to endless verification loops, to the worst case we have personally hit: a challenge page that crashes the AI's desktop app outright. As of August 2026 that failure is real; it may be fixed in an hour or never. If your agent hits a wall: stop, don't retry, and open the site yourself in your own browser — the wall is aimed at your agent, not at you.

What llms.txt can do

llms.txt is information, not enforcement: a site's index and terms for AI readers. A well-behaved agent honors what it declares — including limits ("interpret this carefully", "this content changes fast") — but nothing forces a rude one to. It's the handshake of the friendly end of the spectrum.

Where this site stands

We've done everything we know how to do to make this site easy and friendly for your agent while staying fully human-friendly: open robots.txt with AI access welcomed, llms.txt with honest terms, a Markdown mirror beside every page, no bot-walls, no consent gates, no JavaScript required to read anything. That's the standard the friendly end of the spectrum sets — and it's the point of the whole site.
