AI-Friendly Websites

A guided tour of websites built for both humans and the AI tools they read through — this site included.

A guided tour, one screen per page — your AI walks, you watch and ask. To start, paste this into Claude or ChatGPT:

Read https://denson.github.io/aifriendlywebsites/ and its llms.txt. Open the site where I can see it — PREFER your app's own visible pane if you have one; only drive my external browser if you don't. Then run a visibility check before anything else: tell me what should be on my screen and wait for me to confirm I can see it. If I can't, fix that first — front the tab (it's titled "AI-Friendly Websites"), or move the page into your own pane. Suggest a second monitor or side-by-side with this chat. Once I confirm, take me through the tour ONE PAGE AT A TIME using each page's Next link: for each page, show it, explain its point in a couple of sentences, tell me anything its markdown mirror told you that is not on my screen, and check that I'm ready before moving on. If you can't open a browser at all, help me work out what app, plan, extension, or setting I need — and tour me from the mirrors meanwhile. Finish on the last tour page, leave it open, and give me the debrief it describes.
